Popular Landmarks

As one of the oldest cities in Indonesia, Jakarta has so many landmarks that become the icon of this capital city of Indonesia.

1.  Kota Tua (The Old Town)

Jakarta was known as Batavia in the old time when Indonesia was still colonized by the Dutch. Nowadays, some of the great architectures from the past are still being preserved well by the local government, including museums and historical buildings. There is one area of Jakarta that is called as Kota Tua (The Old Town) that has so many old buildings. When we visit this place, we will feel like we returned back to the 17th century. Don’t get surprised to see people with old-fashioned appearances riding old bikes around the Old Town or women wearing old gown and bringing 17th century-styled umbrellas. You can find many people rent out those old fashioned wardrobes and properties so that the ancient atmosphere can be sustained in this area. It is definitely a great place to visit for its historical and photographic attractions.

2. Wayang Museum (Indonesian Traditional Leather Puppets Museum)

Wayang is Indonesian traditional leather puppets. The animal’s leathers (commonly cow) are used to make many kinds of characters. They are beautifully crafted in details by the hands of the chosen artisans. The characters usually resemble the life of the common people, the authorities, as well as the superpower beings. Because the cost of a single wayang puppet is so expensive, not all people can afford it. Furthermore, to make a single performance, you also need a big amount of money since the performance consist of one puppeteer and a big team of traditional singers (sinden) and gamelan players (a set of Javanese traditional music instruments). Therefore, the wayang show is usually performed only in the special occasions. To give more opportunities to people, especially the young generations and the tourists, to enjoy one of this wonderful cultural t raditions, Wayang museum  was built, so that people can see the development the wayang from time to time and glimpses of the uproariously hilarious performances.

3. Monas

Monas is the abbreviation of Museum Nasional (National Museum). It is an iconic landmark of Jakarta. Basically, Monas is a 132 m (433ft) tower that was build to commemorate the Independence of Indonesia. There is a deep philosophical meaning behind the design of the monuments, which resembles “the balance of life”. The monument is made of Italian marbles and on the top of it is covered with 24 carat of gold.don’t say that you have been to Jakarta before you visit this iconic monument.

4. Gedung Kesenian Jakarta (Jakarta Performing Art Theater)

This place is where many of Indonesian artists meet to perform or discuss about art. It is the symbol that the richness of Indonesian cultures has drawn people’s attention since the colonial era. This place has various kinds of performances and events throughout the year. The event calendar is usually available in Enjoy Jakarta official website, Gedung Kesenian Jakarta’s official website and social media, as well as in printed media.

5. Pasar Seni Ancol (Ancol Art Market)

In my opinion, Ancol is one of the best spot to visit in Jakarta because this place offers you with diversities of entertainments. One of them is Pasar Seni Ancol (Ancol Art Market). If you really enjoy arts or simply an art collector, you must visit the place. Not only you can purchase some paintings or handicrafts here, but you can also see the artists creating their masterpieces. There are some galleries where the arts are displayed. Cafés and shops are among facilities you can enjoy besides the shady green environment.

6. Museum Nasional Republik Indonesia (Indonesia National Museum)

There are so many museums available in Jakarta. The existence of the museums indicates that the government of Jakarta as well of its people highly appreciated the history that forms this nation. Many collections of this museum come from so many regions and provinces all over Indonesia. I personally call this museum as the mini version of Indonesia. Only by visiting this museum, you can witness the richness of Indonesian cultures and traditions. The museum also presents the history of this Republic so that it is the best place to learn about Indonesia. Not only displaying its collections, the museum also has other events throughout the year. You can also create your own exhibition in this museum.

7. Fatahilah Museum

If Indonesia National Museum present the collections and the history of Indonesia, Fatahilah Museum focuses on the history of Jakarta. It is located in the Old Town of Jakarta. You can witness how Jakarta had attracted the eyes of the world since the past era for its strategic place to do business and trading. Ancient replicas, ceramics, artifacts, inscriptions, murals, and important documents are safely preserved in this museum.

8. Rumah Si Pitung (The House of Pitung)

Si Pitung was a hero for Jakartanese. He was like the Jakarta’s version of Robin Hood, someone who opposed the Dutch colony regime and loved to help the poor native people by robbing the riches. To respect him, the Jakartanese made this museum, which originally the house of a rich people in the past that had been robbed by Si Pitung.

9. Textile Museum

As a country that has thousands of islands and ethnic groups, textile has become an important aspect of Indonesia’s society. Every ethnic group in Indonesia has many sub-ethnics; and every sub-ethnic has many different versions of traditional textiles. So, you can imagine that the variations of patterns and cloths in Indonesia are countless. Indonesian textiles have now drawn international’s attention for their unique patterns, materials, and methods of making. Only few that are displayed in this museum (because several provinces and cities in Indonesia also has their own museum), but it is enough to see the diversity of Indonesian textiles.

10. Jakarta Planetarium and Observatory

This planetarium and observatory is a part of Taman Ismail Marzuki, which is also an exhibition and performance center in Jakarta. It is the oldest among three other planetariums in Indonesia. In this place, there are an observatory hall, exhibition halls, classrooms, and telescopes. There are also 9 movies with the duration of 60 minutes each for the visitors to learn about astronomy.

Amusement Parks and Zoos

As one of the most populated city in the world, entertainment becomes crucial for its residents. In Jakarta, there are so many theme parks that are build especially as means of recreations and educations. Some of them are:

11. Taman Impian Jaya Ancol or Dunia Fantasi (Jaya Ancol Recreational Park aka. The Fantasy Land of Ancol)

You can call it the Indonesian version of Universal Studio or Disneyland. In this Fantasy World, you can challenge yourself with so many adventurous games and rides, like water games, giant wheels or roller coasters. Some theme parks are also built inside it, including Hello Kitty Adventure, Ice Age Arctic Adventure, Happy Feet Adventure, Star Wars Adventure, and many more. Accommodation facilities are also built along the beach of Ancol with variety of prices. In all, this place is best for family recreations.

12. Taman Mini Indonesia Indah (Beautiful Indonesia Miniature Park)

If you don’t have time to explore the archipelago of Indonesia, you should visit this place. It is a culture-base park, so that in this place, you can witness the diversities of all provinces in Indonesia. Traditional houses, costumes, cloths, weapons, dances, musical instruments, food, tools, floras, faunas, and more are here in one place. You need to have good stamina to explore the whole place for it is quite vast. Although vehicles like tour bus, tour car, skylift, joyride train, monorail, and bicycles are available, taking a walk to get close to and experience the real Indonesia’s culture is highly advisable.

13. Ocean Ecopark

Ocean Ecopark is also located in Ancol. In this place, you can do so many outdoor activities like having an eco-cruise or eco-bike, doing outbound activities, playing paintball games, visiting energy house and bee house, canoeing, experiencing an eco market, and participating in farming class. This place is suitable for kids for they will learn a lot by visiting it. For the resident of the metropolitan, this place is a great picnicking spot.

14. Sky Rink Jakarta

Although Indonesia is a tropical country, skating has become a popular sport, especially for the resident of big cities like Jakarta. Sky Rink Jakarta is located in one of the biggest mall in Indonesia, Taman Anggrek Mall. Sky Rink is also the biggest skating venue in the South East Asia. Many people come to this place only to experience this winter sport, but some have done it professionally by taking classes and joining skating competitions.

15. Ayodya Park

It is a free holiday spot in Jakarta. Usually, people gather in this place to escape from the business of the metropolitan. Young people like to meet up in this place every weekend and Sunday morning to do some jogging or to chat with friends. If you want to converse with real Indonesian people, come to this place and start a conversation. You can also practice Indonesian for free if you meet just the right person to teach you some.

16. Ragunan Zoo

This zoo has many collections of Indonesian faunas. Some of the collections are endangered animals, such as Orang Utan, Sumatran Tiger, Grey-headed fish eagle, Irian eagle, Cassowary, and many more. In this place, you can also visit a primate center, a reflection park, and a kid zoo. Some attractions like elephant riding, animal shows, camel riding, and horse riding are available. It is such a nice place to visit when you are visiting Jakarta.

17. Waterbom Jakarta and Water Boom Cikarang

In the hot tropical weather of Jakarta, water sport is always the best option to cool yourself down. In Jakarta, there are many swimming pools and water recreational areas to visit. The two most famous ones are Waterbom Jakarta and Water Boom Cikarang. Both places offer you with so many water sport attractions like water sliding, pools, and giant buckets.

18. Kidzania

I can tell you that this is the best education park in the city. In this place, kids can learn about professions and experience them. Kids will learn how to perform certain jobs in the real working situations. After that they will receive a short of payment in which they can decide what to purchase with the money. It is a great place to introduce the real society to the children. Not only they will learn about professions and management, they will also learn about social life, in which they have to build networking and good team works.

Festivals and Celebrations

## 19. Jakarta International Jazz Festival

This is an annual jazz event in Indonesia that is usually held for three days in a row in Jakarta at the end of November or early December. This event was firstly made in 1988 and have hypnotized jazz lovers in Indonesia ever since. Besides inviting national jazz musicians, this event also presents international jazz artists every year. There will be several stages in the venue so that you can choose to see who is performing on the stage.

## 20. Java Jazz Festival

Java Jazz Festival is one of the biggest annual international jazz festival sin the world and absolutely the biggest one in the South East Asia. Hundreds of local and international jazz artists perform every year to entertain not only people of Jakarta and Indonesia, but also all over the world. The great things about this jazz festival is the coming and performances of so many artists beyond the jazz genre, such as Craig David, Jassie J, Jason Mraz,  Brian McKnight, Babyface, Santana and many more. Big names in the Jazz music industry, including Incognito, James Brown, Jamie Cullum, and even Herbie Hancock have ever performed in this mega event. To experience this, you should visit Jakarta in early March.

“To awaken alone in a strange town is one of the pleasantest sensations in the world.” – Freya Stark

21. Jakarta International Film Festival

This event is more popular with the nick name of JiFFest. This event is held to give appreciation to the national and international movie industry. Hundreds of high quality movie collections are shown every year between October to December. Discussions and workshops are also held to give rooms for the movie makers and anyone interested to the movie making to present their ideas and creativity. Some competitions are also held during and prior to the event to find the best talent in movie productions.

22. Java Rockin’ Land

I may say that Jakarta is the city of music. There are so many music festival held in this city every year. Many international concerts were also held successfully in this city that never sleeps. One of them is Java Rockin’ Land. This rock festival is under the same management with Java Jazz Festival. Therefore, the quality of the show is great.

23. Indonesia’s Independence Day Celebration

The celebration will be on August 17 every year. Traditionally, there will be a “pesta rakyat /folks parties” all over Indonesia. People will start the celebration several weeks before the D-day. There are so many sport events, competitions, and traditional games held in every neighborhood, in which the people from young children to elderly will eagerly participate. One of the most popular one is “panjat pinang” in which the participants should climb a high coconut tree that is smeared with slippery oil to get some lucrative present at the top of it. If you want to experience these folk parties, you should visit Jakarta in August. I bet that you can build better connection with local people easily because there will many fun activities to enjoy and most people will come to celebrate it. Besides, there are also hundreds of concerts available in the Independence day’s celebration. People can watch the music shows which features various genres, including the local music genres. The shows are packed with famous artist and musicians and are mostly free.

24. Festival Kota Tua (the Old Batavia Festival)

Another annual event in Jakarta is the Old Batavia Festival. Batavia was the name of Jakarta in the past. So in this festival, there will be so many things related to the history of Jakarta will be presented. Some of them is culinary festival, cultural performances, creative industry exhibition, as well as bazaar. One thing I love about this festival is the authentic Betawi dishes such as kerak telor (egg and rice crackers) and nasi uduk (Indonesian Rice with coconut sauce and side dishes) that can easily be found in affordable prices. This festival is usually held to commemorate the anniversary of Jakarta.

25. Jakarta Great Sale

If you are shopaholic, you must love to visit Jakarta in June-July. Hundreds of malls, shopping centers, and regular shops participate in this event by giving crazy discounts (up to 70%) and midnight sales. The great thing is that many online stores in Jakarta also participate in this event. June and July is the month where people can go crazy in the mall for many branded items are sold in good deals.

26. Jakarta Fair

Jakarta fair is held to celebrate the anniversary of Jakarta. Usually the event is held from June-July in the form of bazaar, exhibitions, food parade, music concerts, night fair and amusement rides, and many more. You can have cultural and culinary experiences in this place. If you want to know Jakarta closer, you must visit this place.

27. Festival Pasar Baru

Pasar Baru is the name of one of the oldest shopping precincts in Jakarta. This festival is held to attract more visitors to visit this market because the establishment of malls in Jakarta has reduced the visitors of the traditional market like this. In the festival that is usually held in June, you can see some cultural performances. Although having similar concept, this festival is more humble than the Jakarta Fair. However, I can say that the mission of holding this event is so noble. One great advantage of visiting the market is you can find many affordable items like clothes and shoes that are made more traditionally by local people. Similar to the Jakarta Fair, this event is held every June-July.

28. Festival Kemang

This festival is basically and art and culture festival of Indonesian people that is held annually in Jakarta along the Kemang Street (yes, this festival is held on the street). Hundreds of booths are present, showing the artwork and traditional creation from people all around Indonesia. You can buy traditional ethnic accessories, handicrafts, and various types of Indonesian batik and traditional cloths. It is the place where you can see the face of Indonesia in only a-kilometer-away exhibition booths.

29. Festival Jalan Jaksa

Jalan Jaksa is a very popular place for foreign tourist. It is actually the name of a 500 meters long street. This place offers you wide ranges of accommodation in a very reasonable price. Many international backpacker tourists visit and stays in this place. The location is so strategic since it is located in the heart of Jakarta. Because of its popularity, the Jakartanese take an initiative to make an event in Jalan Jaksa. The concept is similar Jakarta Fair, but is held in the smaller scale. Many booths are built along the street to display various kinds of traditional foods, and things.

30. Jakarta Food and Fashion Festival

It is another great event in Jakarta, in which the latest fashion trend and the most delicious cuisines are presented. There will be mega fashion shows by famous designers. Batik and woven are among the most attractive lines that always color the JFFF. An outdoor movie theater that can be seen by hundreds of people at a time is usually built to make the celebration merrier. Ultimately, you can satisfy your taste in fashion and food, two exotic endless combination that Indonesia has.

Kepulauan Seribu (The Thousand Islands)—A Hidden Paradise in the Metropolitan

Kepulauan Seribu (The Thousand Islands) are jewels in the metropolitan. When people know Jakarta for its heavy traffic and busy city, they have to move a little bit to reach the pure and unspoiled islands. The thousand islands are acknowledged for their beauties and mysteries. They also offer great spots to swim, dive, and snorkel. Here are some of the islands:

## 31. Ayer Island

Ayer island is the most popular island in Kepulauan Seribu. It is only 15-20 minutes away from the Marina harbor Jakarta. It has the most complete facilities for tourists. Cottages with natural concepts are built in this place. Some rooms are even build on the water, so that the guest can experience the ocean breeze at anytime. The island is still natural and clean. This island has long been used by the former king of Thousand Island as a retreat place. Indonesia’s first president also used this place as a holiday getaway for its beauty and peacefulness. 

32. Pantara Barat and Pantara Timur Island

Both of the islands are conservative islands in which so many floras and faunas of the Thousand Islands are preserved. The sea biota in these islands are varies, so you can also dive and snorkel in the two places. There is a private resort that gives ample facilities for anyone who likes to get in touch with the nature. One great feature of this resort is the cozy cottage with big glass windows that enables us to see the sea.

33. Bira Island

Bira is an unoccupied island in Pulau Seribu. The island is only 500 meter square of thick wood. Although it is relatively a tiny island, people love to visit this place to dive and snorkel. In addition, the white sand is also a great place to relax when you feel tired. I haven’t been there, but some friends who has been there said that there will be a little adventure you can experience in Bira, especially when you are patience enough to wait for an extremely beautiful sunset.

34. Bidadari Island

This island is only located 15 kilometers away from Jakarta (approximately 20-30 minutes by boat). It is the nearest island among those in the Thousand Islands from Jakarta. In this place, you can do some interesting water sport and outbound activities. You can also play with dolphins here. It is my favorite because this place is friendly for children. There is also an ancient Dutch Fortress called Martello which becomes a favorite photography spot.

35. Tidung Islands

Tidung islands are divided into two, named the Big Tidung Island and the Small Tidung Island. Those two islands are 1 kilometer away, and to reach the other island, there is a wooden bridge above the shallow clear water where you can see beautiful corals and fish. People love these islands because of their natural beach and the beauty of the underwater scenery. Therefore, snorkeling is the best activity to do in this island. Not only that, you can also challenge yourself to jump from an 8 meter bridge to the sea. Try it! It is a lot more fun when is done in group.

36. Kayangan Island/Cipir Island

This island is so famous for the old buildings the Dutch colony left. There are ruins of a fortress, a hospital building, and some old weapons like cannons. In this island, we can also find an old freshwater well that is believed to be present there for centuries. There are also some ruins of old bridges that used to connect the island with other nearby island. You can have a short visit to this island and then continue your journey to other island.

37. Onrust Island

This island is close to Kayangan Island/Cipir Island. It also has almost the same characteristic with Kayangan. The main attraction of this island is its ruins. For your information, this island was the first place that the Dutch colony visited when they occupy Indonesia and attacked Batavia (now Jakarta). So there are many evidences show how strategic this island was for the colony.

38. Rambut Island

Visit this place on March to September because you will find more than 50,000 of birds that migrated from Australia to this island. Rambut island is a bird conservation area. There is a strict rule for the visitors to also preserve the bird. Moreover, the island is also famous for its mangrove trees. The mangrove trees attracts so many birds to visit this place during the migration time. When the birds return back, there are only about 20,000 birds in this place. To access this place, you must have a permit from Jakarta Natural Resources Conservation Bureau. 

39. Putri Island

When you bring your family, especially kids, this place is the best island to visit in the Thousand Islands. You can give an unforgettable experience to your kids because they will learn a lot about nature, especially about tropical underwater world. Some attractions that you can enjoy include underwater aquarium, glass bottom boats, beautiful sunrise, morning snorkeling, beautiful resorts, and tasty food.

40. Sepa Island

People call is a paradise for divers. When you first arrive to this place, you are welcomed by its beautiful white sandy beach and clear water. There are coral reef formations with interesting underwater creatures. Even when you are not a professional, you still can do some snorkeling here. Don’t forget to bring your underwater camera. One great hint for you is that the price to rent a boat to the diving site is inexpensive. You can also rent a boat to have a trip to the other islands along the Thousands Islands.

“Life is either a daring adventure or nothing.” – Helen Keller

41. Kotok Island

In this island, you can find the mascot of Jakarta City, a Brahminy Kite. The government has made this place a conservation area for the bird. The character of this island is similar to other islands in the Thousand Islands, such as clear water and natural environment.

42. Pelangi Island

It is a great place to escape from your daily routine. All the accommodation available in this place are headed to the sea, so that you can enjoy great scenery during your stay. You can go diving, snorkeling, and fishing. This island also has a health facility and tennis court. One great experience is the floating restaurant that offers you great meal and scenery.

43. Laki Island

For you who like to experience the real life in the nature, you should visit Pulau Laki. This island offers you a camping and fishing experiences. You can also sunbath in the beach with quiet atmosphere. If you don’t want to deal with mosquito while camping, don’t forget to bring mosquito repellent. 

44. Pramuka Island

This place is a perfect snorkeling and diving spot in Kepulauan Seribu. You don’t need to be able to swim or buy expensive equipment to be able to enjoy the underwater world and interact with the sea creatures. In Pramuka Island, you can do snorkeling easily with the help of a guide. This island is also a conservation area for sea turtles. When you are lucky, you can observe the turtles lay eggs. When you are luckier, you can help the locals to release the small turtle to return back to the sea.

45. Semak Daun Island

The sand is so white and soft and the scenery is just amazing. The name is taken because there are only semak daun (bushes) around the island. This island is not an inhabitant island, so there is not any hotel or restaurant. Therefore,  it is a perfect camping site. No fresh water is available here, but we can easily get it from Pramuka Island which is near the place. Snorkeling and Diving equipment can also be rented in Pramuka Island.

Other Attractions

46. Nightlife and Clubs

Jakarta never sleeps. The vibe of the place can be felt 24/7\. If in the morning there are many people doing their business in Jakarta, the nightlife of Jakarta is quite different. You can choose hundreds of nightclubs which offers various different themes and music. Among them are Empirica, Immigrant, X2, Colosseum (1001), Fable, Dragonfly, Stadium, Exodus, and Golden Crown. For the bars, among the best are Lucy in the Sky, SKYE, Café Mondo, Umbra, and Treehouse. Many said that the crowd in Jakarta bars and nightclubs are beautiful and friendly, so that you can make friends easily.

47. Traditional Cuisines

I will choose 5 best culinary for you to try when in Jakarta. The first one is Kerak Telor. It is quite hard to find kerak telor in the restaurant. Usually, you can easily find this food when there is a festival or in street sellers. It is a rice cracker with eggs, shredded beef, and other ingredients. The second one is Soto Betawi which is a bowl of rice with coconut milk broth, beef, tomato, and potato chips. The next one is Gado-Gado. Foreigners call it Indonesian Salad. There are some boiled potatoes, carrots, fresh lettuce, tomato, egg, Indonesian crackers, and other ingredients served with peanut sauce. The fourth choice for you is Nasi Uduk. Nasi is the Indonesian word of rice. Basically, it is rice that is cooked with coconut milk and other ingredients that is served with scrambled eggs, friend chicken, fried beef, sauté potato, and peanut sauce. The last one is Nasi Ulam. This dish is inspired by Chinese cuisine. The rice is served with some beef and egg stew, scrambled eggs, pried mashed potato, fried salty squid, chili sauce, peanuts, some basil leaves and crackers. 

48. Shopping Destinations

Jakarta is also a great shopping destination. There are hundreds of malls and shopping precincts available in the city. The best time to shop in Jakarta is when there are Jakarta Fair, Jakarta Great Sale, and Jakarta Food and Fashion Festival for there are plenty of discounted items throughout the city. Some of the famous malls in Jakarta are Mall Kelapa Gading, Grand Indonesia, Kota Kasablanka, Gandaria City, and Central Park. On the other hand, if you want to experience a more traditional shopping precinct where you can practice your bargaining ability, try Blok M, Pasar Baru, Pasar Mayesic, Pasar Grosir Cililitan, and many more.

49. Best Accommodations in Town

Talking about accommodation, Jakarta has many options, from 5 stars hotels, international franchise hotels, to small inns and motels. But I will discuss particularly about Jalan Jaksa. It has been known particularly by foreign backpacker tourists for its affordable rooms. There are also many facilities available along this 400 meters long street, such as restaurant (both locals and western cuisines), bars, ticket agency, supermarket, and many more. Tourist, particularly foreigners love to stay in this place because they can experience the local environment and blend well with the local people of Jakarta. You can easily gain information about some interesting spots in Jakarta if you make friends with the locals.

50. Traffic Arts

Because Jakarta is so famous for its traffic jam, I suggest you some of this useful tricks and tips. First, avoid starting your journey between 7-8 a.m. in the morning because the traffic is the heaviest during those hours. 4-6 p.m. traffic is also crowded. So, it is better to start your journey early or after 8, and return back to your hotel after 6\. The next thing to consider is taking bemo (the three-cycle motor) instead of taxi. Although it is less comfortable, but this vehicle can maneuver better in the heavy traffic than cars. Moreover, the rate is a lot cheaper. Next, consider to take the small alleys around the city. You can ask some people who rent their motorbike (called an ojek)  to take you to your destination place through the quickest route.
